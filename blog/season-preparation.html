Building the block

The preparation period is designed around clarity. Each athlete knows the purpose of the week, the key performance indicators, and the recovery habits that protect consistency. This page can be duplicated for news, event recaps, interviews, or training reports.

The content structure supports long-form writing while keeping the layout easy to scan. Subheads, short paragraphs, pull quotes, and side panels give editors enough room to tell a real story.

Performance improves when the plan is specific enough to guide action and simple enough to repeat under pressure.

What the week includes

  • Two high-intensity sessions with clear output targets.
  • Technical work focused on repeatable movement quality.
  • Recovery monitoring, sleep notes, and nutrition check-ins.
  • Short review sessions after key training days.
